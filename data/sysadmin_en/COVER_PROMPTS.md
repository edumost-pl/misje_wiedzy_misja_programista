# Cover prompts — Mission Admin (Amazon, legally clean)

Generated draft art (no title text baked in):

- `assets/covers/cover-back-mission-admin.png`

Use these prompts for **front** and **back** covers. They are written to avoid trademarked logos, official character marks, and branded game packaging.

## Legal rules baked into the art brief

**Do NOT show on the cover art:**
- The word “Minecraft” as baked-in text in the image
- Creeper face, Enderman, Steve/Alex skins, or other distinctive character marks
- Official game logos, app icons, or store badges
- Exact official block-texture atlases used as trade dress
- Raspberry Pi Foundation logo lockups (the board itself is OK as a product photo subject)
- Microsoft / Mojang logos

**OK to show:**
- A real Raspberry Pi board on a desk
- A child (about 11) with headphones
- A monitor with a **softly blurred**, generic **blocky colorful voxel world** (not readable UI, not logo)
- Warm daylight, premium kids-nonfiction look (DK / Usborne energy)

**Title & subtitle go in the design layout (InDesign / KDP), not inside the AI image.**

Suggested Amazon title stack (layout text, not in the PNG):
- **Title:** Mission Admin  
- **Subtitle:** Raspberry Pi, Linux & Your Own Game Server · Ages 10–12  
- **Spine:** Mission Admin  
- **Disclaimer line (back, small):** Unofficial educational guide. Not affiliated with Mojang Studios, Microsoft, or the Raspberry Pi Foundation.

### Back cover copy (layout text — English)

A friend is waiting to join your world. Mission Admin takes kids 10–12 from a credit-card-sized computer to a private home game server: Linux, the terminal, files, networks, and safe setup with an adult. Works at home with a Raspberry Pi — and in class without one.
